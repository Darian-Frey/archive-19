SCP Archive

Secure. Contain. Protect.

This archive holds original SCP Foundation articles, written for personal interest. The articles use the SCP format and setting but are not canonical entries on the SCP Wiki.

To begin, select an item from the index. A common thread runs through the collection: institutions and ordinary people encountering the limits of what they can know about themselves and the universe, and the cost — borne by individuals — of holding that knowledge.

Not every article is dark. Some are about the responsibility of having found something good.
