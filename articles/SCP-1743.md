# SCP-1743

**Item #:** SCP-1743

**Object Class:** Apollyon

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-1743 cannot be contained. SCP-1743 is a property of the universe as a whole, and the Foundation is inside it. Containment procedures address the *suppression* of the finding, the *welfare* of personnel exposed to it, and the *maintenance* of the instrument that produced it.

The VESTIGE apparatus, originally constructed at the ████████ Institute for Subatomic Physics (a civilian national laboratory in ███████), has been acquired by the Foundation under cover of a funding withdrawal and relocated to Site-██. It is operated under **Project Meridian**, administered by the Department of Anomalous Physics. A second, independently constructed apparatus (VESTIGE-II) is maintained at Site-██ in the southern hemisphere for replication purposes. Both instruments are run once per calendar month. The results are logged. The results have never varied from prediction.

The nine members of the original ████████ research team have been retained under Foundation employment where willing (six), pensioned under nondisclosure where not (two), and in one case placed under continuing medical care (see Addendum 1743-F). No member of the team has been administered amnestic treatment. The Ethics Committee's reasoning on this point is recorded in Addendum 1743-G. It is not a reasoning the Committee enjoyed producing.

All raw VESTIGE data, and in particular the **boundary dataset** (Addendum 1743-E), is stored offline at Site-██ under Level 4/1743 clearance. Personnel are not to examine the boundary dataset for more than four cumulative hours without a mandatory interval of fourteen days. This limit was established empirically.

Disclosure of SCP-1743 beyond Project Meridian, the O5 Council, and the Ethics Committee is prohibited. The O5 Council has determined that the Foundation's mission is unaffected by SCP-1743. The Council's phrasing was: *"Secure. Contain. Protect. We have been doing this for seventeen years. We will continue."*

---

**Description:** SCP-1743 is the finding, established by direct measurement and independently replicated, that **the universe came into existence 17 years, 4 months, 3 days, 13 hours, and 9 minutes before the first VESTIGE measurement**, with a margin of error of approximately forty seconds, and that it did so **already in progress** — with a complete physical history of approximately 13.8 billion years already in place, already consistent, and already underway.

The figure increases at a rate of one second per second. As of the filing of this document, the universe is approximately 19 years old.

The Foundation refers to the instant of the universe's actual beginning as **the Onset**. It refers to the entire physical record apparently predating the Onset — cosmological, geological, biological, historical, and personal — as **the Backfill**. The Backfill is complete. The Backfill is internally consistent. The Backfill is, by every test other than VESTIGE, indistinguishable from a history that happened.

It did not happen. Nothing did, before 14:51:07 UTC on a Tuesday in ████ 20██.

## Addendum 1743-A — VESTIGE

The **Vacuum Elapsed-State Interferometric Experiment** (VESTIGE) was constructed between 20██ and 20██ at the ████████ Institute to test a prediction of the so-called *chronometric vacuum* class of beyond-Standard-Model theories. These theories hold that the quantum vacuum, in addition to its known properties, carries an accumulated phase that has advanced at a fixed rate since the vacuum's formation in the electroweak epoch. The phase itself is unrecoverable — it is a number modulo 2π, and could be anything — but the *decoherence envelope* around it is not. The width of the envelope is, in the theory, a direct function of the total proper time the vacuum has been in existence.

VESTIGE was designed to measure the envelope by interfering a beam-generated virtual-pair population against a reference superconducting cavity. The theory predicted that for a vacuum 13.8 billion years old, the envelope would be so wide as to be unresolvable — the instrument would return a flat, structureless result, consistent with an effectively infinite elapsed time. This was the expected outcome. The experiment's purpose was to place an upper bound on a coupling constant. It was, in the words of its principal investigator, "a null result we could publish."

VESTIGE's first full-power run took place on ██ ████ 20██. It returned a sharply resolved envelope.

The envelope corresponded to an elapsed time of **5.47 × 10⁸ seconds**.

The team spent eleven days searching for the error. The principal investigator, **Dr. Anneliese V██████**, later described this period in a Foundation debrief:

> *We assumed it was the cavity. Then we assumed it was the beam. Then we assumed it was the software. We replaced the cavity. We recalibrated the beam. We rewrote the analysis pipeline from nothing, in a different language, on a different machine, and had it reviewed by someone who had not seen the first version. We ran it again.*
>
> *It came back nine days older.*
>
> *That was the moment. Not the first result. The first result was a number, and a number can be wrong. The second result was a number that was exactly nine days, four hours and some minutes larger than the first, and the interval between the runs was exactly nine days, four hours and some minutes. It wasn't measuring a fault. It was measuring a clock. And the clock had started seventeen years ago.*

Dr. V██████ contacted a former colleague, then a Foundation embedded asset at a European funding council, "because I needed to tell somebody who wouldn't think I was mad, and I couldn't think of anyone else." The Foundation took possession of the facility eight days later.

## Addendum 1743-B — Replication

The Foundation has verified SCP-1743 by the following means.

**1. Original apparatus, relocated.** VESTIGE was disassembled, moved to Site-██, reassembled, and run. The result was consistent with the original measurements, advanced by the elapsed interval.

**2. Independent apparatus.** VESTIGE-II was constructed at Site-██ by a separate team working from the theoretical papers only, without access to VESTIGE's engineering drawings or software. Its first run returned a result consistent with VESTIGE to within thirty-one seconds.

**3. Location independence.** The measurement has been performed at sea level, at 4,100 metres, in a deep mine, in a sealed vault whose contents had been undisturbed since 1911, and aboard an aircraft at altitude. The result does not vary. There is no location in the universe, so far as the Foundation has been able to test, that is older than the Onset.

**4. Monthly runs.** Both instruments have been run monthly since acquisition. The result has advanced, on every occasion, by precisely the interval since the previous run. The universe is keeping time.

**5. Theoretical review.** Foundation physicists have examined the chronometric-vacuum framework for any mechanism by which VESTIGE could return a finite, small, steadily-increasing elapsed time for a universe that was in fact 13.8 billion years old. None has been found. The instrument does what it was built to do. The universe is what it measures.

## Addendum 1743-C — The Backfill

The consequences of SCP-1743 for the physical record are total, and are summarised here without elaboration, because elaboration has not been found to help.

- The cosmic microwave background was created at the Onset, already at 2.7 K, already redshifted, already imprinted with the acoustic structure of a plasma that never existed.
- Every photon arriving at Earth from a source more than approximately 17.3 light-years distant was created *in transit* at the Onset, a few light-years short of arrival, with its full journey already applied to it. The Andromeda galaxy has never been observed. Light that was made to look as though it came from Andromeda has been observed.
- The geological record, the fossil record, radiometric decay products, ice cores, tree rings, sediment layers, and the isotopic composition of every object on Earth were created at the Onset in the state they would have been in had the Backfill occurred.
- Every written record, every photograph, every archive, every building, every scar, every grave, and every person over the age of approximately nineteen was created at the Onset, in progress, with a history that did not take place.
- Every human memory of any event before 14:51:07 UTC on the day of the Onset is a memory of the Backfill. The memories are real. The events are not.
- The Foundation's own archive, and every anomaly it records as having been contained prior to the Onset, is Backfill. The Foundation, as an institution, is nineteen years old. So is everything else.

The Department of Anomalous Geography has noted, in a memorandum appended to this file, that SCP-3340 — previously believed to be a network at least 4,400 years old — is nineteen. The Department reports that this has not made the phenomenon easier to understand, and has not made them feel better.

## Addendum 1743-D — The Onset

Project Meridian has back-calculated the instant of the Onset to **14:51:07 UTC, ±40 s, on ██ ████ 20██**. It was a Tuesday.

The Foundation has examined every available record — seismic, atmospheric, electromagnetic, astronomical, financial, telecommunications, and Foundation-internal — for any discontinuity at or near that instant. There is none. Nothing was recorded. Nothing was noticed. No instrument glitched. No one reported a lost moment, a doubled moment, a sense of having come in halfway through a sentence. Approximately eight billion people experienced the afternoon of that Tuesday as continuous with its morning.

The morning did not take place.

Project Meridian has been unable to find *anything* — any event, birth, death, experiment, transmission, or alignment — that distinguishes that instant from any other. Its choice, if it was a choice, appears to have been arbitrary. The Foundation has, with some reluctance, concluded that this is not a gap in the investigation. It is a finding.

> *We assumed at the start that the Onset would correlate with something — that a universe does not begin on a Tuesday afternoon for no reason. We now think that it does. Or rather: we now think that the reason, if there is one, is not located in anything we can see, and that the universe was not started* at *anything, or* for *anything, that is inside it.*
>
> *It was not started for us. The Backfill is not a deception, because a deception needs someone to be deceived, and the Backfill is equally complete in places no one will ever look. The far side of every exoplanet is furnished. The interior of every star is furnished. Every grain of every sediment on every seabed on every world is furnished to the same fidelity as the memories in my head. None of it is pointed at us. We are not the audience. We are part of the set.*
>
> — Dr. V██████, Project Meridian internal memorandum

## Addendum 1743-E — The Boundary Dataset

VESTIGE resolves the decoherence envelope with sufficient precision that its *edge* — the point corresponding to the Onset itself — can be examined in detail. Project Meridian refers to the data at and immediately around this edge as the **boundary dataset**.

Beyond the boundary, the instrument returns no value. Not zero. Not noise. No value: the instrument has been pointed at something it has no representation for.

At the boundary, the data is not clean.

A clean beginning would present as a step: nothing, then something. The boundary dataset instead presents a small, structured region approximately 2.1 seconds wide in elapsed-time units — an interval of the Onset itself, during which the envelope is neither absent nor established — and within that region the data has *structure*. The structure is not random. It is not periodic. It does not correspond to any known physical process. Foundation analysts who have examined it have described it, in their reports, as "a seam," "a signature," "the residue of an operation," and — in one report subsequently withdrawn by its author at his own request — "a thumbprint."

The Foundation does not know what the structure is. It has established the following:

- The structure is identical in VESTIGE and VESTIGE-II to the limit of resolution.
- The structure is stable across every monthly run. It does not evolve.
- The structure is *non-compressible*: it contains no redundancy that any analysis has been able to exploit, which is characteristic either of true randomness or of information in a format the analyst does not have.
- The structure is not random.

Personnel who examine the boundary dataset closely and for extended periods report a consistent experience, which the Project Meridian medical officer has summarised as follows: *"an escalating conviction that the structure is legible, that it is on the verge of resolving into something the observer would recognise, and that the observer very much does not want it to."* No personnel has reported the structure resolving. The four-hour exposure limit was established after the second such report and has been sufficient, in all but one case, to prevent lasting effect.

## Addendum 1743-F — Personnel

Of the nine members of the original ████████ team:

Six have continued in Foundation employment under Project Meridian. They are, by the medical officer's assessment, "as well as can be expected, which is to say: functional, careful, and not entirely present." Four of the six have children. Two of those four have children who are older than nineteen.

Two have accepted pensions and left physics. One now keeps bees. The other has not responded to correspondence since 20██, though she is confirmed alive and residing at her registered address.

One, **Researcher T████**, aged twenty-six at the time of the first run, requested and was granted extended access to the boundary dataset during the initial characterisation, before the exposure limit was established. He spent an estimated one hundred and ninety hours with it over three weeks. He was found on the morning of the twenty-second day sitting in the VESTIGE control room with the display switched off, calm, and unwilling to speak.

Researcher T████ remains under continuing care at Site-██. He is physically well. He eats, sleeps, and walks in the grounds. He does not speak, read, or write, with one exception: if asked, in writing, what the current elapsed time is, he will write a figure in years, months, days, hours, minutes, and seconds. He does this without consulting any clock. He has been asked two hundred and eleven times. He has never been wrong by more than the instrument's own margin.

He has, on three occasions, written the figure unasked. On each occasion the figure was correct, and was followed by a second line. The three second lines read:

> *it is still running*
>
> *it is still running*
>
> *the seam is on the outside*

The following was recovered from Dr. V██████'s personal notebook during a routine security review. She was informed of its recovery, and asked whether she wished it to be excluded from the file. She said she did not. It is reproduced without alteration.

> *Day 40.*
>
> *I have been alive for seventeen years. My daughter is twenty-four. She is older than the universe. She is older than me.*
>
> *I keep trying to catch the Backfill out. I try to remember the morning of the Tuesday, and I can. I had a meeting at ten. I remember what I ate. That is the problem: I cannot find the join. The memory of the morning is exactly as good as the memory of the afternoon. It is exactly as good as the memory of my wedding, which did not happen, and of my daughter's birth, which did not happen, and of my own childhood, which was furnished, at 14:51 on a Tuesday, in the same operation that furnished the interior of the sun.*
>
> *Day 46.*
>
> *Amnestics won't help. I asked. Amnestics take away memories of things that happened. There is nothing to take. If they wiped me back to the Onset I'd be left with seventeen years of real memory and nothing before it and that would be the truth and I don't think anybody could live in the truth. The Backfill is the thing that lets us stand up. I understand that now. I don't think it was put there for that. I think that's just what it does.*
>
> *Day 51.*
>
> *Looked at the boundary again. Two hours. Shouldn't have.*
>
> *There is a shape in it. Not a shape. The place where a shape was. It's the imprint of whatever was holding this when it was set down. And it's on the outside. T████ is right. The seam is on the outside, and we are on the inside, and there is no reason to think that whatever set this down has looked at it since. Why would it. You don't look at the set. You don't look at the inside of the thing.*
>
> *Day 52.*
>
> *I was calm about it all day and then this evening I realised: the Backfill is finished. It was finished at the Onset. It was made whole and then it was set running, and everything since is unfurnished. Us. These seventeen years. We are the only part of the universe that has ever actually happened. We are the first draft, running live, and nobody made us to be looked at, and nobody is looking.*
>
> *I used to find the sky frightening in the ordinary way — the size of it. It's much smaller now. It's seventeen light-years across and then it's painted on. That should be a comfort. I'm writing it down because I want to remember that it wasn't.*
>
> *Day 60.*
>
> *Back at work. Ran the monthly. Sixty days older. It's fine. It's a clock. You can live next to a clock.*

Dr. V██████ continues to lead Project Meridian. She runs the monthly measurement personally. She has not examined the boundary dataset since Day 51.

## Addendum 1743-G — Ethics Committee Finding

The Ethics Committee convened in 20██ to consider three questions: whether SCP-1743 should be disclosed within the Foundation; whether exposed personnel should be administered amnestics; and whether the VESTIGE instruments should be destroyed.

The finding is reproduced in part.

> *On disclosure. The Committee finds that SCP-1743 changes nothing about what the Foundation does, and everything about what the Foundation is. The anomalies are real. The containment is real. The nineteen years are real. The mission does not require that the institution be older than it is. The Committee recommends against disclosure, not because personnel could not bear it, but because there is nothing they could do with it, and the Committee has watched what it does to people who have nothing to do with it.*
>
> *On amnestics. The Committee has considered the use of Class-A amnestic treatment on the original team and on Project Meridian personnel. The Committee notes that amnestics remove memories of events. The Committee further notes that, for any personnel member over the age of nineteen, the memories at issue are among the only memories they possess of events that occurred. The Committee is not willing to remove from a person the only true things in their head in order to leave them more comfortable with the false ones. The Committee acknowledges that this is a philosophical position, and that it has been arrived at under some strain.*
>
> *On the instruments. The Committee has considered destroying VESTIGE and VESTIGE-II and sealing the file. The Committee notes that this would not alter the age of the universe. It would only mean that the Foundation no longer knew it. The Committee has, for the whole of its institutional existence — all nineteen years of it — held that knowing is preferable to not knowing, and finds that it is not prepared to abandon that position at the one point where it is being tested.*
>
> *The instruments will be maintained. The clock will be read. Someone should be keeping the time.*

The finding was adopted. The O5 Council concurred by a vote of 11–2.

## Addendum 1743-H — Note

A display in the Project Meridian control room at Site-██ shows the current elapsed time since the Onset, to the second, derived from the most recent VESTIGE run and advanced by the site clock. Personnel refer to it as **the uptime**. It is not required by any protocol. It was installed at Dr. V██████'s request.

The following note is appended to this file by the Project Meridian lead, and is to be retained in all future revisions.

> *This document records that the universe is a certain age, and gives the date on which that was first measured. If you are reading this and the elapsed time since that date is greater than the uptime — if the universe is younger than this file — then there has been another Onset since this was written, and this document, and Project Meridian, and VESTIGE, and the nine of us, and everything described here, are Backfill: furnished in place, in progress, complete, and never having occurred.*
>
> *We would have no way of knowing. Neither would you. Run the instrument anyway.*
