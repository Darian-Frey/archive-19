# SCP-2001

**Item #:** SCP-2001

**Object Class:** Apollyon

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-2001 cannot be contained. SCP-2001 is, by the current working understanding of the Foundation, **the condition under which containment itself takes place.**

What can be contained — and what these procedures address — is **the knowledge of SCP-2001**, and the cognitive and institutional consequences of that knowledge propagating within the Foundation.

Awareness of SCP-2001 is restricted to **Tier-Ω** clearance, currently held by forty-one (41) Foundation personnel across all sites. Tier-Ω clearance is not requestable, nor declinable. Personnel are inducted into Tier-Ω only after the Department of Resilient Cognition has determined, with high confidence, that the candidate has *independently arrived at the conclusion of SCP-2001 by their own reasoning* and is suppressing it. The Foundation does not teach the conclusion. The Foundation acknowledges, and contains, those who have already learned it.

Tier-Ω personnel are subject to **the Atlas Protocol**: continuous psychological monitoring, structured peer support, restricted access to high-energy experimental apparatus, and — under conditions specified in Addendum 2001-G — voluntary cognitive amelioration via supervised retrograde amnestic treatment. Approximately 31% of Tier-Ω personnel have, over the program's history, requested amelioration. Most have, after amelioration, eventually re-derived the conclusion and re-entered Tier-Ω, on average within nineteen months. The Foundation does not currently believe amelioration is curative. It believes amelioration is a kindness.

The Foundation does not, under any circumstances, attempt to disseminate awareness of SCP-2001 beyond Tier-Ω. The Foundation does not, under any circumstances, attempt to disseminate awareness of SCP-2001 to the general public. The Foundation acknowledges that this policy is, in some respects, a betrayal of its founding charter, and has accepted the betrayal as the cost of operations.

---

**Description:** SCP-2001 is the designation given, after approximately forty-six years of accumulated evidence, to the **conclusion** that the planet Earth is an enclosed environment under sustained external observation, and that the SCP Foundation, as an institution, exists *within* that enclosure, in a containment relationship with one or more external agencies operating at a higher level of reality than the one accessible to its personnel.

In ordinary language: **the Foundation is contained.** The Earth is the container. The anomalies the Foundation has spent its history studying are, in this framework, not principally external threats to be neutralised but **features of the enclosure itself** — diagnostic instruments, experimental stimuli, byproducts of the containment apparatus, or possibly, in some cases, attempts at communication.

The Foundation is the contained party. It does not, by present evidence, know who is doing the containing, why, or for how long the arrangement has been in effect. It has, however, accumulated sufficient evidence that the arrangement *exists* to classify the underlying condition as Apollyon, on the grounds that no containment effort the Foundation can undertake is, in principle, capable of changing it.

The classification is also Apollyon for a second reason, recorded in Addendum 2001-H: **the knowledge of SCP-2001, once acquired by an individual, cannot be reliably suppressed, removed, or contained at the institutional level over a long time horizon.** Every generation of senior Foundation researchers, given sufficient years and access to the relevant evidence, independently arrives at the same conclusion. The Foundation does not contain the truth. The Foundation contains the *consequences* of the truth.

## Addendum 2001-A — The Earth Question

The earliest known reference to what would later be designated SCP-2001 is a single sentence in a 1971 internal memorandum by Dr. ████████, then Director of the Department of Statistical Anomalies, addressed to the O5 Council. The memorandum's primary subject was a routine resource-allocation request. The sentence was a parenthetical aside in the third paragraph:

> *(I would also like to note, without recommending any action, that the geographic distribution of our active case files continues to be statistically incompatible with any cosmological model in which anomalies arise uniformly throughout the universe. I do not have an alternative model to propose. I mention it only because the discrepancy has now persisted for the entirety of the Foundation's operational history.)*

The memorandum received no formal response. Dr. ████████ retired in 1978 and died of natural causes in 1989. The memorandum was archived under standard procedures and not reviewed again until 2003, at which point a junior researcher cross-referencing historical caseload data flagged it for the attention of his supervisor. His supervisor, Dr. R████████, read the parenthetical and, by her later account, "sat for approximately twenty minutes without moving, then asked for the rest of the day off." She returned the following morning. She did not raise the matter again for six years.

The Foundation refers to this entire class of observation as **the Earth Question**, defined as follows:

> *Why does the totality of anomalies known to the Foundation appear to be located on, originating from, directed at, or relevant to the planet Earth and its inhabitants, to a degree that is statistically incompatible with any uniform distribution of anomalous phenomena throughout the observable universe?*

The Earth Question, in this form, has been asked — and resisted being answered — by senior Foundation personnel since at least 1971. The Foundation's standard institutional response has been to treat the question as a methodological artefact: of course the Foundation finds anomalies on Earth, because the Foundation *is* on Earth. The data are selection-biased. The question is malformed.

This response is, on close examination, insufficient. The Foundation has, since 1962, operated an unmanned anomaly-detection program targeting locations throughout the solar system and, since 1991, throughout the local stellar neighbourhood. The program has detected, in sixty-four years of continuous operation, **zero (0)** confirmed anomalies outside the immediate vicinity of Earth.

This is the result the Foundation expected. It is also, on reflection, the most significant single piece of evidence in the SCP-2001 case file. The universe, as observed from Earth, is **mundane.** It runs on physics. It contains stars, gas, dust, and the predictable products thereof. The exceptions are all *here.* Within a radius of approximately one Earth diameter, the laws of nature have approximately fourteen thousand documented exceptions. Outside that radius, they have, as far as the Foundation has been able to detect across six decades of looking, **none.**

A reasonable person, reviewing this evidence without institutional commitments, would arrive at a small number of possible explanations. The Foundation has, internally, enumerated them. They are listed below.

1. The universe is genuinely uniform, and the Foundation's extrasolar detection methods are inadequate. *(Status: Cannot be ruled out, but the methods have been refined over six decades and consistently produce null results. The hypothesis is increasingly strained.)*
2. Anomalies exist throughout the universe but are unstable, and only persist on Earth due to some local property of Earth's environment. *(Status: No candidate local property has been identified. Anomalies are known to function in vacuum, in microgravity, and under varied physical conditions, and have been transported off-Earth and returned without losing anomalous properties.)*
3. Anomalies arise from a property of consciousness, mind, or observation, and are therefore concentrated wherever conscious observers are concentrated. *(Status: Plausible, but not falsifiable, and does not explain the existence of anomalies that predate human consciousness or that operate in the absence of observation.)*
4. The universe is **not uniform**, and Earth is a region in which exceptions to physical law are deliberately or incidentally concentrated. *(Status: The hypothesis the Foundation has spent forty-six years trying not to adopt.)*

If hypothesis 4 is correct, the next question is *why.* The Foundation has, in its private deliberations, identified three sub-hypotheses, each of which is independently distressing.

- **4a (Reserve):** Earth is a preserved environment, maintained in its current state for reasons unknown. Anomalies are byproducts of preservation infrastructure. The Foundation, in containing them, is performing maintenance on a system it does not own.
- **4b (Laboratory):** Earth is an experimental environment. Anomalies are stimuli, instruments, or test conditions. The Foundation's containment activities are themselves data points in an experiment the Foundation cannot perceive.
- **4c (Cage):** Earth is a containment environment. Something inside it is being held. The Foundation's instinct to contain anomalies is, in this framing, a *symptom of being contained* — an institutional reflex inherited from whatever set up the enclosure.

The Foundation does not currently know which of 4a, 4b, or 4c is correct. The Foundation does not currently know whether any of them is correct. The Foundation has, however, allocated significant resources, over multiple decades, to attempting to **find out.**

## Addendum 2001-B — The Slow Pattern

Between 1971 and approximately 2009, the Earth Question circulated within the senior Foundation hierarchy as a private intellectual concern — discussed in oblique terms during retirement dinners, hinted at in technical papers, never formalised. This period is referred to within the Department of Resilient Cognition as the **Long Silence.** It was the longest period of institutional self-deception in the Foundation's history.

The Long Silence ended for institutional reasons rather than evidentiary ones. In 2009, the Foundation's caseload, which had grown approximately linearly since the organisation's founding, **changed slope.** The change was small — an increase of approximately 4.7% in annual anomaly detection rates — and could have been attributed to improved detection methodology. It was not. Subsequent statistical analysis, conducted between 2011 and 2014, established that the rate change was real, was global, and had no identifiable terrestrial cause.

The Foundation was detecting more anomalies because there were more anomalies. The anomalies were not arising from any new natural process the Foundation could identify. They were arising as if they were being **introduced.**

The Long Silence ended in a meeting of the O5 Council on ██/██/2014, at which Dr. R████████ — by then Director of Statistical Anomalies — presented the rate-change data alongside a forty-three-year retrospective on the Earth Question. The meeting's full transcript is sealed. The portion that has been declassified, for inclusion in this document, consists of a single recorded exchange.

> **O5-█:** You are telling us, after forty years, that we have been contained.
>
> **Dr. R████████:** I am telling you that the evidence is consistent with that hypothesis. I am not telling you it is the only hypothesis.
>
> **O5-█:** What are the other hypotheses?
>
> **Dr. R████████:** They are worse.

The Council voted, 11-2, to formally designate the Earth Question as an active research priority and to allocate resources accordingly. The designation **SCP-2001** was assigned at the same meeting. The two dissenting votes are recorded but not attributed. One of the dissenters was, by all available evidence, dissenting on the grounds that the project should not be undertaken at all. The other dissenter is believed to have been arguing for a more aggressive response. The Council has not, in the twelve years since, clarified which was which.

## Addendum 2001-C — The Lighthouse Experiment

Between 2014 and 2019, the Foundation designed, resourced, and constructed the experimental apparatus formally designated **Project Lighthouse**, located at a facility identified in this document only as **Site-██.**

Project Lighthouse was the Foundation's first and only attempt to **directly test** hypothesis 4: that Earth is a region in which exceptions to physical law are deliberately or incidentally concentrated, and that the boundary of the enclosed region might be, in principle, observable.

The full technical specification of Project Lighthouse remains classified. The operational principle, summarised for the purposes of this document, was as follows:

Anomalous physics, by Foundation definition, consists of phenomena that violate the local rules of natural law as observed elsewhere in the universe. If the local rules are *enforced* — that is, if some external mechanism maintains them — then sufficiently extreme tests of the rules might cause the enforcement mechanism to **become detectable**, either through observable correction (the rules being actively maintained against perturbation) or through observable failure (the rules briefly lapsing under load).

Project Lighthouse was designed to apply such a test. The specific physical regime targeted, and the magnitude of the energies involved, are described in technical annexes not reproduced here. It is sufficient to note that Project Lighthouse required, at peak operation, energy throughput on the order of █████████ watts, sustained for approximately ████ seconds, in a configuration that the Foundation's senior physicists assessed as "the most dangerous experiment ever performed by a human institution, including all known nuclear weapons tests."

The experiment was conducted on ██/██/2019.

The results are described in Addendum 2001-D.

## Addendum 2001-D — The Glimpse

Project Lighthouse was conducted under a strict observation protocol. Thirty-one personnel were present in the primary observation chamber. An additional 104 personnel monitored remotely from secondary stations. All observation surfaces — instrument readouts, optical sensors, redundant recording devices — were synchronised to a master timecode accurate to within nanoseconds.

The experiment ran for ████ seconds. Initial analysis of the recorded data, conducted in the days immediately following the experiment, indicated that the apparatus had functioned as designed and that no anomalous signature had been detected. The experiment was logged as a successful null result. The personnel were debriefed. The facility was put into standby.

Three days later, an instrumentation specialist conducting routine quality-assurance review of the optical sensor logs flagged an anomaly. At timecode 00:02:14.886 — approximately fourteen seconds before peak energy throughput — sixteen of the twenty-three optical sensors had recorded an event lasting **0.0007 seconds.** The event did not register on the lower-resolution instrument readouts that the observation team had been monitoring in real time. It had been missed.

The event, when reconstructed from the sixteen synchronised optical records, was a **field of view.**

The Foundation has, in subsequent years, debated the proper technical description of what the optical sensors recorded. The minimal characterisation, agreed upon across multiple independent analyses, is the following: for 0.0007 seconds, the optical sensors recorded data that was consistent with **the apparatus being briefly observed from outside.** Not "observed from outside the chamber." Not "observed from outside the facility." **Observed from outside the regime of physical law within which the apparatus was operating.**

The visual content of the observation, reconstructed from the sixteen sensors, is described in subsequent Foundation documents using a single word: **attention.** The sensors recorded the presence of attention. The attention had a *direction*. The direction was *down*, in a sense of "down" that does not correspond to any spatial axis the Foundation's physics describes. The attention was held for 0.0007 seconds, and then released.

No further events were detected during the remainder of the experiment, or in any subsequent operation of the apparatus.

The instrumentation specialist who first identified the event, Researcher M█████, completed his quality-assurance review, filed his report, and went home. He did not return to work the following day. He had, by all subsequent evidence, intended to. He was found by his partner in the early afternoon, sitting in his garden, conscious and unharmed, unable to speak. He spoke again three weeks later. His first sentence was: *"It noticed us. It noticed us and then it stopped noticing us. I have been thinking about which was worse."*

Researcher M█████ is currently a Tier-Ω researcher. He has not requested amelioration. He has stated, on multiple occasions, that he prefers to remember.

## Addendum 2001-E — The Twenty-Eight

Of the thirty-one personnel present in the primary observation chamber on the day of the Lighthouse Experiment, **three** independently reported, in the days following, having "noticed something odd" during the experiment that they had been unable to articulate at the time. The three reports were filed separately, were not coordinated, and were initially dismissed as expectable post-experimental confabulation.

The remaining **twenty-eight** personnel reported no anomalies. They were debriefed, signed off on the null-result conclusion, and returned to ordinary duties. They were, by every available measure, fine.

In the seventy-eight months following the experiment, **fourteen** of the twenty-eight have spontaneously, and without external prompting, asked their supervisors questions structurally equivalent to the Earth Question. The questions have been asked in different forms — a comment over dinner, an aside during a review, a half-joking remark at a conference — but the Department of Resilient Cognition's retrospective analysis has determined that all fourteen are recognisably the same question, and that all fourteen were asked by personnel who, at the time of asking, did not consciously recall the Lighthouse Experiment.

The Foundation's working interpretation is that the optical sensors were not the only instruments to record the 0.0007-second event. The personnel present recorded it too. They recorded it without knowing they were recording it. The recording is now *integrating*, in a process that the Department of Resilient Cognition describes as "delayed cognitive consolidation of pre-articulate observation," and which Tier-Ω researchers describe, less formally, as **"the slow knock."**

Of the fourteen who have arrived at the Earth Question, **nine** have been inducted into Tier-Ω. **Four** have requested and received amelioration; two of those have re-derived the conclusion and been re-inducted. **One** committed suicide before the Foundation became aware of his cognitive trajectory. He left a note. The note is reproduced in Addendum 2001-I.

The remaining **fourteen** of the twenty-eight personnel have shown no relevant symptoms. They are under continued passive observation. The Department of Resilient Cognition does not expect this to remain stable.

## Addendum 2001-F — The Cognitive Profile of Awareness

The Department of Resilient Cognition has, over twelve years of monitoring Tier-Ω personnel and adjacent populations, developed a structured profile of how the awareness of SCP-2001 affects different cognitive types. The profile is included here in summary form because it informs the Atlas Protocol and because, frankly, Tier-Ω personnel reading this document deserve to know what to expect.

**Type 1 — Withdrawal.** The most common response (approximately 41% of confirmed cases). The subject becomes quieter, more thoughtful, less invested in ordinary social and professional activity. They continue to function. They are competent. They are simply *elsewhere*. Withdrawal is the response the Foundation prefers, on operational grounds, and is the response that retrograde amnestic treatment is most reliable at addressing.

**Type 2 — Acceptance.** Approximately 22%. The subject integrates the knowledge into their working model of the world and continues operating with relatively little disruption. Type 2 responders often become the most effective Tier-Ω personnel — they are the ones who design containment protocols, draft documents like this one, and conduct the long, careful research that the program requires. They are, however, also the ones most likely to develop **secondary symptoms** over time, including a slow but measurable loss of interest in the Foundation's containment mission. Their reasoning, when articulated, tends to converge on the same point: *if we are contained, then containing is what is being done to us, and to do it ourselves is to collaborate.*

**Type 3 — Evangelism.** Approximately 14%. The subject feels a compulsion to share the conclusion with others. The compulsion is not malicious; it is, in their own accounts, a sense of moral obligation. *People deserve to know.* Type 3 responders are the most operationally dangerous to the Foundation's containment of SCP-2001, and are managed through structured engagement, peer pressure, and — in three documented cases — induced isolation. None have been amnesticised against their will. The Foundation has, however, denied two requests for retirement on these grounds. The denials are recorded in the Foundation's internal ethics ledger as compromises.

**Type 4 — Signal.** Approximately 11%. The subject becomes preoccupied with *responding* to the entity glimpsed during the Lighthouse Experiment. They want to communicate. They want to be acknowledged. The cognitive profile resembles, in some respects, the religious instinct, though Type 4 responders typically reject the religious framing when offered it. The Foundation has, on three occasions, allowed Type 4 personnel to design and conduct small-scale signalling attempts under controlled conditions. The signalling attempts have produced no detectable response. The Type 4 responders involved have nonetheless reported, in all three cases, a sense of *relief* — as if the act of attempting had been the point. The Foundation has tentatively classified Type 4 as the response least likely to result in operational compromise and has, with some hesitation, begun to view it as a desirable adaptation rather than a pathology.

**Type 5 — Collapse.** Approximately 8%. The subject cannot integrate the knowledge. They suffer acute psychological deterioration — typically depression, dissociation, paranoid ideation, or, in the most severe cases, suicide. The Foundation has lost six personnel to Type 5 collapse since 2014. The Department of Resilient Cognition's standing recommendation is that any researcher displaying Type 5 indicators be offered immediate amnestic treatment, and that the offer be repeated until accepted or until the trajectory stabilises. The recommendation is followed. It is followed, in the Department's own internal note, *too often* — meaning that Type 5 is overdiagnosed by clinicians who would rather amnesticise a colleague than watch them suffer.

**Type 6 — Adaptation.** Approximately 4%. The subject reports, after a period of integration, that the knowledge has become *unremarkable* to them. It is something they know, the way they know other things. It does not preoccupy them. It does not distress them. They continue their work. They report no diminished function. They are, frankly, indistinguishable from Type 1 responders except that, when asked, they say they have not withdrawn — they have simply *finished thinking about it.*

Type 6 responders are the rarest profile. They are also the profile the Department of Resilient Cognition watches most closely. The reason is not that they are dangerous. The reason is that the Department does not, in its own assessment, understand them. The Department's standing question, recorded in its annual report, is whether Type 6 represents a healthy adaptation that the rest of the Tier-Ω population should aspire to, or whether it represents something the Department is failing to recognise.

The current Director of the Department, Dr. F████, is herself Type 6. She is aware of this. She has noted, dryly, that she is not in a position to evaluate her own classification objectively, and that the Department should perhaps not be run by someone in its most ambiguous category. She has, nonetheless, continued to run it. The O5 Council has not relieved her. The Foundation has, in this matter as in others, chosen the imperfect option that allows operations to continue.

## Addendum 2001-G — The Atlas Protocol

The Atlas Protocol is the standing containment programme for Tier-Ω awareness. It is named for the mythological figure who held up the sky, and the name was chosen, by the program's first director, with the explicit understanding that Atlas's task was eternal, unrewarded, and that he was not the one being protected.

The protocol consists of:

1. **Continuous monitoring** of Tier-Ω personnel for symptom progression, conducted by Department of Resilient Cognition clinicians with full disclosure of the program's purpose.
2. **Structured peer support**, in which Tier-Ω personnel meet in small groups (typically four to six members) on a biweekly basis. The meetings are unstructured by design. Tier-Ω personnel are permitted to discuss SCP-2001 freely within these groups. The groups are not recorded.
3. **Restricted access** to high-energy experimental apparatus, on the grounds that several Type 4 responders have, historically, attempted to repeat or extend Project Lighthouse without authorisation.
4. **Voluntary cognitive amelioration** via supervised retrograde amnestic treatment, available on request, with no professional penalty for accepting or declining.
5. **Memorial protocols** for Tier-Ω personnel who die by any cause, including a sealed personal letter — written by the deceased in life and retained against this purpose — to be opened by the surviving members of their peer group. The letter typically contains the deceased's reflections on SCP-2001 and any final thoughts they wished to share. The Foundation does not read these letters. The peer group does. The Foundation has determined, after considerable internal debate, that this is the correct policy. The letters are not, and will not be, part of the official record.

The Atlas Protocol is administered by an annual budget of approximately ████████ USD, funded from the Foundation's general operations account under the cover line "Personnel Wellness Services." The line item has not been challenged in twelve years. The Department of Resilient Cognition believes this is because the personnel responsible for budgetary oversight have, in disproportionate numbers, themselves become Tier-Ω.

## Addendum 2001-H — Why Apollyon

The classification of SCP-2001 as Apollyon was debated for approximately eighteen months following the Lighthouse Experiment. The argument *against* Apollyon was procedural: by Foundation convention, Apollyon-class anomalies are those for which containment will, with certainty, eventually fail. SCP-2001 has no containment to fail. The condition simply *is*. Apollyon, the argument went, was a category error.

The argument *for* Apollyon was the one that ultimately prevailed. It rests on a reframing of what is being contained.

SCP-2001, properly understood, is not a phenomenon to be contained. It is **the awareness of a phenomenon, propagating within a population of skilled investigators who have access to the relevant evidence.** The Foundation employs, by design, the kind of people who will eventually arrive at the Earth Question. The Foundation requires such people in order to conduct its primary mission. The Foundation cannot, without ceasing to be the Foundation, restrict access to the evidence or filter for personnel incapable of drawing the conclusion. Therefore, the Foundation will, with certainty, eventually employ Tier-Ω-eligible personnel faster than it can induct, manage, or amnesticise them.

The current Tier-Ω population is forty-one. The Department of Resilient Cognition's projections, extrapolated from the last decade of inductions, indicate that this number will exceed **one hundred** by 2034, **five hundred** by 2050, and the entire Foundation senior research population by approximately **2080.**

At that point, the Foundation will be, in effect, an institution of universally aware personnel attempting to contain the knowledge of its own contained status from the institution it is part of. This is, in the Department of Resilient Cognition's quiet assessment, **not a stable configuration.** The Foundation will, before then, either find a new institutional posture toward SCP-2001 or cease to function in its current form.

The Apollyon classification reflects this. Containment, in any meaningful sense, will fail. It is failing now. The Foundation is managing the rate of failure, not the fact of it.

This is, in retrospect, what Apollyon has always meant.

## Addendum 2001-I — The Note

Researcher T████, one of the twenty-eight personnel present at the Lighthouse Experiment, took his own life on ██/██/2021, approximately twenty-two months after the experiment. He had not, prior to that date, been identified by the Department of Resilient Cognition as a Tier-Ω candidate. He had not, by any available record, articulated the Earth Question to anyone. He had been, by all accounts, well.

He left a note. The note was found on his desk. It is reproduced in full below.

> *I am sorry.*
>
> *I have been trying to write this letter for several months. I have not been able to make it shorter. I am writing it now, in the form it has reached, because I do not have any more time to refine it.*
>
> *I do not know what I saw. I know that I saw something. I know that I did not see it with my eyes, because my eyes were on the instruments, and the instruments were not where the thing was. I saw it anyway. I do not know how. I have read the technical reports. I do not believe the technical reports describe what I saw. I believe the technical reports describe what the apparatus recorded. The thing I saw was not in the apparatus. The thing I saw was in the room. The thing I saw was looking at the room. The thing I saw was looking at me.*
>
> *I do not think it meant any harm. I do not think it meant anything in particular. I think it was, very briefly, paying attention. I think the experiment caused it to pay attention. I think when the experiment ended, it stopped paying attention. I think it has not paid attention since, and I think it will not pay attention again, unless we do something to attract it.*
>
> *I have not been able to stop thinking about whether we should.*
>
> *I think the answer is no. I think if it paid attention to us again, for longer, it would notice things it did not notice the first time, and I think some of those things would be us. I think being noticed is the worst thing that has ever happened to me. I think being noticed for longer would be worse. I think being noticed *deliberately*, by a thing that decided to notice us, would be worse than that.*
>
> *I am writing this so that the next person who is asked to design the next experiment will have my opinion on file. I do not have the standing to formally oppose the experiment. I have the standing to write this letter.*
>
> *I would like, very much, for someone to know that I tried to leave a record. I would like for the record to be read by someone who understood what I was saying without my having to explain. I have not, in my life, found very many such people. I hope this letter finds one.*
>
> *I am sorry I could not stay. I tried. I would like the people who knew me to know I tried.*
>
> *Please do not do the experiment again. Please. Please.*

The Foundation has not, in the five years since Researcher T████'s death, repeated the Lighthouse Experiment. The apparatus remains operational. It is not scheduled for use. The current Director of Project Lighthouse, who is Tier-Ω and Type 4, has stated for the record that she will not authorise its operation during her tenure.

She has also stated, for the record, that she is approximately 60% certain her successor will. She does not know when her tenure will end. She has not requested amelioration. She has stated that she would like to remain aware for as long as the Foundation will let her, because she is, in her own words, *the only person currently in the building who is going to argue against the next experiment, and someone needs to be.*

The Foundation has accepted this.
