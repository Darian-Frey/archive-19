# SCP-7244

**Item #:** SCP-7244

**Object Class:** Safe (provisional — see Addendum 7244-D)

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-7244 is stored in a standard anomalous-object containment locker at Site-19, Sub-Level 3, on a felt-lined wooden pedestal. The locker is kept at ambient temperature and humidity. No special atmospheric, electromagnetic, or radiological precautions are required.

Personnel are not to look at SCP-7244 for longer than ninety (90) seconds in any twenty-four hour period. Personnel are not to photograph SCP-7244 without optical filtering (see Addendum 7244-B). Personnel are not to attempt to determine, by handling, counting, or tracing, the number of faces, edges, or vertices of SCP-7244.

The maker of SCP-7244, PoI-7244 (Mr. D████ H██████, retired electrician, of ████████, Vermont), is under Class-B monitoring and remains at his place of residence. He has been informed that his creation has been acquired by a private collector. He believes this. He is content.

Under no circumstances is PoI-7244 to be provided with additional woodworking materials beyond those he already possesses, nor is he to be reminded of the dream that preceded SCP-7244's creation. Foundation personnel embedded in his community (one neighbour, one pharmacist, one member of his weekly card group) are instructed to redirect conversation if the topic arises.

---

**Description:** SCP-7244 is a hand-carved wooden object measuring approximately 14 cm in its longest dimension, weighing 213 grams, and constructed from a single piece of seasoned black walnut (*Juglans nigra*). The wood is unremarkable. The tool marks are consistent with hand carving using standard chisels and gouges. The finish is a single coat of boiled linseed oil.

The shape of SCP-7244 cannot be described.

This is not a stylistic claim. Foundation personnel attempting to describe SCP-7244 in writing produce descriptions that are internally consistent but mutually incompatible. Personnel attempting to sketch SCP-7244 produce drawings that, when overlaid, do not depict the same object. Personnel attempting to construct a 3D model of SCP-7244 in CAD software produce models that fail mesh-validation: the models contain faces that share edges with themselves, vertices with negative valence, and — in two documented cases — volumes whose computed interior is larger than their computed exterior.

SCP-7244 is, by every available measurement, **a three-dimensional physical object occupying three-dimensional physical space.** It has a mass. It has a volume (213 grams ÷ 0.65 g/cm³ ≈ 328 cm³, consistent with its apparent size). It casts a shadow. It can be held. It can be set down.

It cannot, however, be a shape.

## Addendum 7244-A — Acquisition

SCP-7244 came to the Foundation's attention on ██/██/20██ via a routine sweep of regional craft-fair photography. A photograph posted to the ████████ County Agricultural Fair's public album showed PoI-7244's table of hand-carved decorative objects (bowls, spoons, a small carved bear) with SCP-7244 displayed prominently at the centre. The automated visual classifier flagged the image for review, having returned a confidence score of 0.00 across all 4,096 trained object categories — a result the system had not previously produced.

A field agent was dispatched. PoI-7244 was found to be friendly, talkative, and entirely unaware that anything was unusual about his carving. The transcript of the initial interview is reproduced in part below.

> **Agent ████:** Can you tell me about this piece?
>
> **PoI-7244:** Oh, that one? That's the dream one. I had a dream about it, must be six, seven months ago now. Couldn't get it out of my head. Sat down with a block of walnut one Saturday and just — carved it. Took me about three weekends.
>
> **Agent ████:** What is it?
>
> **PoI-7244:** What is it? It's the shape. From the dream.
>
> **Agent ████:** Can you describe the shape?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(laughs)* Well, I mean, it's that. *(points)* You're looking at it.
>
> **Agent ████:** Yes, but if you were describing it to someone who hadn't seen it. Over the phone, say.
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(pauses for approximately forty seconds)* You know, that's funny. I'd say — well, I'd say it's got a — *(pauses)* — I'd just tell them to come over and look at it. I don't think I could tell you. I knew exactly what it was supposed to be when I was making it, but I couldn't tell you now.
>
> **Agent ████:** Did you work from a drawing?
>
> **PoI-7244:** No, no, just from memory. The dream was very clear.
>
> **Agent ████:** Did you find the carving difficult?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(pauses)* That's the funny thing. It was easy. Every cut, I knew where to put the chisel. Like the wood already knew. I just took off the parts that weren't it.

PoI-7244 sold SCP-7244 to the agent for forty-five dollars. He offered to throw in a wooden spoon. The agent accepted the spoon. The spoon is non-anomalous and has been returned to PoI-7244's household via a staged "found in the truck" recovery.

## Addendum 7244-B — Observation Notes

The ninety-second observation limit was established following the experience of Dr. ████, who was the first Foundation staff member to handle SCP-7244 under controlled conditions. Her field notes, recorded by voice memo as she examined the object, are reproduced below with timestamps.

> **0:00** — Object in hand. Heavier than expected. Walnut, definitely walnut, you can smell it. Surface finish is good, hand-rubbed, not lacquered.
>
> **0:14** — Trying to orient it. There's a — I want to say a base, but it doesn't have a base. Every face I set it on, it sits flat. That's unusual. Let me try that again.
>
> **0:31** — Every face. Every single face, it sits flat. I'm putting it down on what I thought was a curved surface and it's *sitting flat.* Okay.
>
> **0:48** — I'm trying to count the faces. I keep losing count. Not because there are too many — there aren't, it's a small object — but because I keep going past where I started and not recognising it.
>
> **1:02** — Going to stop counting. Going to just trace one edge with my finger. Starting at — okay, starting here.
>
> **1:19** — I'm back where I started. I think. The edge doesn't end. I traced it for — what, fifteen seconds? — and I'm back at the start, but I never turned a corner. I never went around anything. The edge is a loop but it's a *straight* loop.
>
> **1:31** — Setting the object down. Stepping back. I'm going to —

The recording ends here. Dr. ████ stepped back from the table, sat down, and remained silent for approximately eleven minutes. When she resumed speaking, she requested that the object be removed from her sight, which was done. She subsequently completed her shift without incident and has returned to normal duties. She reports no lasting effects. When asked, three days later, to describe SCP-7244, she said: *"It was a nice piece of woodwork. I couldn't tell you what it looked like. I don't think it looked like anything."*

The observation limit was set at ninety seconds based on Dr. ████'s recording, which suggested that effects became substantial at approximately the eighty-second mark. Subsequent personnel observations have confirmed this threshold. Below ninety seconds, personnel report only "an unusual carving." Above ninety seconds, personnel begin reporting tracing experiences similar to Dr. ████'s.

No personnel have been harmed. All reported effects resolve within minutes of looking away.

**Photographic note:** Direct photographs of SCP-7244 produce images that, when displayed, induce mild nausea and disorientation in approximately 30% of viewers. The effect is attributable to the camera's attempt to render a three-dimensional shape that does not project consistently onto a two-dimensional sensor; the resulting images contain perspective errors that the human visual system cannot resolve. Photographs taken through a polarising filter set at 45° to the principal axis (an axis SCP-7244 does not, strictly speaking, possess, but which can be approximated) produce stable, viewable images. All Foundation documentation uses such images.

## Addendum 7244-C — The Dream

PoI-7244 was interviewed a second time, under cover of a follow-up "appraisal" by a fictitious private collector, regarding the dream that preceded SCP-7244's creation. The relevant transcript is reproduced in full.

> **Agent ████:** You mentioned the dream. Could you tell me about it?
>
> **PoI-7244:** Sure, sure. It wasn't a long dream. I was in a — I want to say a room, but it wasn't really a room. It was a workshop. Not mine. Bigger. And there was a table, and on the table was the shape. The shape I carved.
>
> **Agent ████:** Was anyone with you?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(pauses)* You know, I want to say yes, but I never saw them. There was someone behind me. Working at another bench. I could hear them. Same kind of work — chisel on wood, you know that sound. They were carving something too. I never turned around to look.
>
> **Agent ████:** Did they speak?
>
> **PoI-7244:** No, no. They were just working. But I had the feeling — and this is going to sound funny — I had the feeling they were *teaching* me. Not by talking. Just by being there. Like when my father taught me to drive. He didn't say much. He just sat in the passenger seat and let me figure it out.
>
> **Agent ████:** And the shape on the table.
>
> **PoI-7244:** That was the lesson. I was supposed to look at it until I understood it. And then I was supposed to go home and make one.
>
> **Agent ████:** Did you understand it?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(long pause)* I understood it then. I don't understand it now. But I remembered how to make it. The hands remembered, you know? Sometimes the hands remember what the head forgets.
>
> **Agent ████:** Have you had the dream again?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(pauses)* No. But I — okay, this is going to sound funny too. Sometimes when I'm falling asleep, I hear the chisels. The other person. Still working. I think they're still there. I think they're working on something else now. Something bigger.
>
> **Agent ████:** Do you want to know what it is?
>
> **PoI-7244:** *(pauses for approximately twenty seconds)* No. I think — no. I think one was enough. I think if they wanted me to know about the next one, they'd show me. They haven't shown me. So I'm leaving it alone.

The second interview was the last substantive contact the Foundation has had with PoI-7244 regarding SCP-7244. He has not, in the eighteen months since, attempted to carve another anomalous object. His subsequent work has been entirely conventional — bowls, spoons, a series of decorative birds. The birds are quite good.

## Addendum 7244-D — Reclassification Petition

Dr. ████, who originally handled SCP-7244, has twice petitioned for its reclassification from Safe to Euclid. Both petitions have been denied by Site Director ████ on the grounds that SCP-7244 has demonstrated no active anomalous behaviour, no boundary-of-containment incidents, and no harm to personnel beyond mild transient disorientation. It sits on its pedestal. It does what objects do.

Dr. ████'s most recent petition, submitted ██/██/20██, contains the following passage, which is reproduced here without endorsement:

> *I am not arguing that SCP-7244 is dangerous. I am arguing that the framework under which we classify it is inadequate. Safe-class objects are objects we understand well enough to contain. We do not understand SCP-7244. We have contained it because it is not trying to leave. That is not the same thing.*
>
> *The object on the pedestal is the easy part. The hard part is the question PoI-7244 has been polite enough not to ask, which is: who was the other person in the dream? PoI-7244 carved an object that cannot exist, and he carved it correctly on the first attempt, from memory, having been shown it once by someone he did not see. He is a competent amateur woodworker. He is not a topologist. He could not have invented this shape. He was given it.*
>
> *Someone is teaching woodworking classes in the dreams of retired Vermont electricians. They are good at it. Their student carved an impossible object on his second try at the chisel after thirty years of bowls and spoons, and the student is fine, and the student is not asking questions, because the student is — and I say this with no condescension — a kind and uncurious man who is happy in his garage.*
>
> *I am asking the question on his behalf. Who is the teacher. What are they teaching. Why this student. Why this shape. Why now.*
>
> *Reclassification to Euclid would, at minimum, require us to maintain a list of woodworkers in adjacent counties and monitor for similar productions. We are not currently doing this. I think we should be.*

The petition is under review.

**Addendum 7244-E (added ██/██/20██):** On ██/██, a hand-carved wooden bowl was submitted to the Foundation's regional intake desk in ████████, New Hampshire, by a field agent following up on a craft-fair photograph. The bowl was carved by a retired postal worker who reports having had "a funny dream about woodworking" approximately four months prior. The bowl is, by every available measurement, an ordinary bowl.

Its interior volume, however, is 1.7 litres.

Its exterior volume is 1.4 litres.

Reclassification of SCP-7244 to Euclid has been approved. Dr. ████'s monitoring list has been authorised.

It currently has nine names on it.

It is growing.
