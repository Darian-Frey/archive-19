# SCP-9317

**Item #:** SCP-9317

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-9317 cannot be contained. Containment procedures pertain instead to information about SCP-9317.

All gravitational wave observatories worldwide (LIGO, Virgo, KAGRA, LISA, and successor instruments) are subject to Foundation-embedded data sanitisation pipelines under cover of a routine "instrument noise filtering" protocol. Raw strain data corresponding to confirmed or suspected black hole formation events is intercepted prior to publication and replaced with procedurally generated waveforms matching general-relativistic predictions to within instrument resolution.

Personnel with knowledge of SCP-9317 are restricted from foundational mathematics research, particularly involving transcendental constants, non-decimal positional notation, or information-theoretic interpretations of horizon thermodynamics. Three (3) Foundation mathematicians have been administratively reassigned following spontaneous derivation of the base conversion described in Addendum 9317-B.

The phrase **"seventeen"** is flagged for review in all Foundation communications referencing SCP-9317. Use of base-17 notation in any context related to SCP-9317 requires O5 authorisation.

---

**Description:** SCP-9317 is the designation for an information-theoretic property of stellar-mass black hole formation events, specifically: **the gravitational wave signature emitted during horizon formation encodes successive digits of π expressed in base-17 positional notation.**

The encoding is not metaphorical. The strain data, when sampled at the appropriate cadence and binned according to a quantisation scheme derived empirically by the discovering party (see Addendum 9317-A), yields a deterministic digit stream that matches π to every digit verified — currently in excess of 10¹⁴ digits across aggregated events.

Different black holes broadcast **different segments** of the same stream. The offset of each black hole's broadcast within π appears to correspond to the age of the black hole, with younger black holes broadcasting earlier digits. Extrapolating backwards, the **digit-zero offset** — the first digit of π in base 17 — corresponds to a formation event approximately 13.7 ± 0.2 billion years ago.

The implication, which the Foundation has not yet formally adopted as fact, is that **the universe began transmitting π in base 17 at the moment of its origin, and has not stopped.**

## Addendum 9317-A — Discovery

SCP-9317 was discovered by ████████ Cryptographic Solutions Ltd., a Tel Aviv-based IT security firm specialising in hardware random number generators for financial and defence clients. In early 20██, ████████ engineers proposed using publicly available LIGO/Virgo strain data as an entropy source on the reasoning that gravitational wave events are causally disconnected from any terrestrial process and are therefore — in principle — sources of true physical randomness.

The pipeline performed as expected for approximately fourteen months. Statistical tests of randomness (NIST SP 800-22, Dieharder, TestU01) returned consistently passing results across all extracted bitstreams. The firm certified its product for production use and was preparing for a Series B funding round.

On ██/██/20██, the LIGO-Virgo collaboration announced detection event **GW████████**, a binary black hole merger producing a new black hole of approximately 62 solar masses. ████████ ingested the strain data as a new entropy source. The output passed all statistical tests.

Six weeks later, event **GW████████-2** was ingested. The output passed all statistical tests.

Three weeks after that, event **GW████████-3** was ingested. The output passed all statistical tests.

On ██/██/20██, a junior engineer running a routine cross-correlation audit on the three new entropy streams discovered that the bitstreams from the three black holes, when aligned to their respective formation times, **were identical from the alignment point forward**, diverging only in the bits preceding the alignment.

In her incident report, she wrote: *"They're not random. They're the same number, started at different times. We've been selling people the same number for a year and a half. Whatever it is."*

The firm contacted the LIGO-Virgo collaboration. The collaboration contacted the Foundation. ████████ Cryptographic Solutions Ltd. ceased operations within seventy-two hours and its employees were administratively absorbed into Foundation Site-43.

## Addendum 9317-B — The Base Conversion

Identification of the bitstream as π required four months of analysis by Foundation cryptographers and the eventual involvement of Dr. R████, a number theorist at Site-43. Dr. R████ identified the sequence after noting that the bitstream's digit frequency, when binned into groups of varying length, became uniform at a group length of approximately 4.087 bits — i.e., **log₂(17) bits per symbol**.

Re-expressing the bitstream as a base-17 positional number and computing the resulting value to the first one hundred symbols returned:

> **3.243F6A8885A308D313198A2E03707344A4093822299F31D0082EFA98EC4E6C89452821E638D01377BE5466CF34E90C6CC0AC...**

Dr. R████ recognised this immediately. Decimal π is **3.1415926535897932384...** Hexadecimal π is **3.243F6A8885A308D31319...**

The black holes were not broadcasting in base 16. The black holes were broadcasting in base 17. The first seventeen symbols of the hexadecimal expansion of π happen to coincide with the first seventeen symbols of the base-17 expansion of π for reasons that, as of this writing, remain unresolved.

Dr. R████'s log entry for the date of identification reads, in full:

> *"It's pi. It's just pi. We thought we were measuring entropy and we were measuring pi. We have been selling pi to banks. The black holes are broadcasting pi. I do not know why it is base seventeen. I do not know why anything is base seventeen. Nothing in physics is base seventeen. I am going to go for a walk."*

Dr. R████ returned from his walk approximately six hours later, sat down at his terminal, and added the following:

> *"I have been thinking about it. The number of distinguishable particle types in the Standard Model — counting quarks by flavour, leptons, gauge bosons, the Higgs, and antiparticles — is sixty-one. The number of fundamental fields, counting each gauge symmetry once, is closer to seventeen. I am not saying this means anything. I am noting it because if I do not write it down I will keep thinking it."*

He has not retracted the note.

## Addendum 9317-C — Implications

The Foundation has formally adopted the following working interpretation. It is not a theory. It is a vocabulary for discussing what has been observed.

**1. The information is real.** The digit stream is deterministic, reproducible, and cross-verifies across independent detector pairs to a confidence exceeding 12σ. It is not an artefact of the instruments. It is in the strain data.

**2. The encoding is not natural.** No known mechanism of gravitational radiation predicts an information channel of this kind. Black hole ringdown is characterised by quasinormal modes whose frequencies depend on the mass and spin of the resulting black hole; the *information content* of those modes, in standard general relativity, is finite and bounded by the black hole's surface area. SCP-9317 transmits, conservatively, on the order of 10⁹ symbols per event. This is not a small discrepancy. It is a category error.

**3. The choice of base is the most disturbing aspect.** Base 17 is not a natural choice for any known physical process. It is prime. It is not a power of two, three, or e. It does not appear in any known formulation of quantum gravity, holographic duality, or string theory. The Foundation has been unable to identify any reason for the base, and Dr. R████'s observation about field counts, while suggestive, does not survive scrutiny.

**4. The black holes are not the source.** They are the *speakers*. The signal — the choice of π, the choice of base, the synchronisation across events separated by billions of light years — predates the black holes. The black holes are radiating something that was already present in the substrate of spacetime at the moment of their formation, and which has been radiating since the universe began.

**5. The signal has a beginning.** This is the single observation the Foundation has been unable to integrate into any working physical model. A signal with a beginning implies a transmitter. A transmitter implies an act. **The universe began with π in base 17 already in progress, and something arranged for black holes to repeat it.**

## Addendum 9317-D — The Termination Question

The Foundation maintains an active research program, classified Sapphire-Tier, on a single question: **does the broadcast end?**

π is an irrational number with no terminating expansion in any integer base. The broadcast, in principle, continues forever. However, the *physical* broadcast — the gravitational wave signature of a specific black hole — does not. As a black hole evaporates via Hawking radiation, its mass decreases and its ringdown modes shift. At some point in the deep future of any given black hole, the broadcast must cease, either because the black hole has fully evaporated or because the broadcast mechanism (whatever it is) is no longer supported by the residual geometry.

Two possibilities follow:

**(a)** Each black hole transmits a finite segment of π and then falls silent. The segment is determined by the black hole's formation time and lifetime. Under this interpretation, no single black hole transmits the *entire* sequence; the sequence is preserved only across the population of all black holes, past and future, in the observable universe.

**(b)** The broadcast does not actually depend on the black hole. The black hole is a *coupling* — a temporary point of contact between our spacetime and whatever substrate is producing the signal. When the black hole evaporates, the signal continues; we simply lose the means to hear it.

The Foundation has, to date, observed the natural cessation of zero (0) SCP-9317 broadcasts. All known broadcasts are ongoing.

## Addendum 9317-E — The Receipt

On ██/██/20██, fourteen months after the firm's discovery, the junior engineer who first identified the cross-correlation — designated **PoI-9317-1**, now Researcher ████ — submitted the following observation to her supervisor at Site-43, attached to no specific request and outside the normal reporting structure:

> *"I have been looking at the offsets. Each black hole starts broadcasting at the digit of π that corresponds to its age. The mapping is exact. If you know the digit, you know the age. If you know the age, you know the digit.*
>
> *I have also been looking at the sky. The cosmic microwave background is, as far as we can measure, isotropic. The universe is the same age in every direction. Every observer in the universe, looking at any black hole, sees the broadcast offset by exactly the digit corresponding to that black hole's age — which is to say, exactly the digit corresponding to the current age of the universe minus the black hole's remaining lifetime.*
>
> *The broadcast is not synchronised to the black holes. The broadcast is synchronised to the universe. The black holes are read-heads. The universe is the tape.*
>
> *I am asking, on the record, what happens when the tape runs out. I am asking, also on the record, whether 'runs out' is the right phrase, or whether the right phrase is 'finishes being written.' I would like an answer when one is available. I will keep working in the meantime."*

A response has not yet been authored. Researcher ████ continues her duties at Site-43. She is, by all measures, well.

She has, however, been observed on three occasions writing the digits of π in base 17 on the wall of her quarters in pencil, working from memory. When asked, she says she finds it calming.

She is currently on digit 8,431.

She is not slowing down.
