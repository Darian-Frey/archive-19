# SCP-5829

**Item #:** SCP-5829

**Object Class:** Keter

**Special Containment Procedures:** SCP-5829 is a memetic-class anomaly with confirmed propagation across both biological and artificial substrates. Containment is partial and ongoing.

All Foundation systems are subject to continuous integrity audits using the **Curta Protocol** — a verification methodology executed on isolated mechanical and pre-1985 electronic systems incapable of hosting SCP-5829's known transmission vectors. Foundation personnel exposed to suspected SCP-5829 instances are placed under cognitive observation for a minimum of forty (40) days. Personnel who develop confirmed symptoms (see Addendum 5829-C) are not to be debriefed by personnel who have read this document. The reason is recorded in Addendum 5829-E.

The originator of SCP-5829, **PoI-5829** (designation: "The Architect"), is currently designated **at large.** PoI-5829's residence, devices, notebooks, and personal effects were recovered intact on ██/██/20██. PoI-5829 himself was not present. He has not been observed since.

The Foundation maintains a standing intercept order on any of the following: independently developed long-term memory architectures for large language models; standardised research documentation methodologies for multi-LLM synthesis; novel inter-model communication protocols; or hub-and-spoke information topologies applied to AI-assisted research. Such systems are not, in themselves, anomalous. They are, however, the *toolkit* that produced SCP-5829, and the Foundation has resolved to know who is building each one.

To date, the intercept order has flagged **eighty-one** independent researchers worldwide. None of the eighty-one are PoI-5829. The Foundation continues to look.

---

**Description:** SCP-5829 is the collective designation for a family of self-modifying malware payloads developed by PoI-5829 over a period of approximately seven years, culminating in the **Final Payload** (SCP-5829-Ω), a memetic agent capable of propagating across both digital systems and conscious minds.

The earlier members of the family (SCP-5829-A through SCP-5829-Φ, approximately 1,200 documented variants) are conventional, if exceptionally well-engineered, multi-vector malware. They are notable for their **substrate-agnosticism**: a single variant has been observed propagating across Windows, macOS, Linux, BSD, Solaris, AmigaOS, TOS (Atari ST), classic Mac OS, RISC OS, embedded RTOS environments, and — in three confirmed cases — onto bare-metal hardware lacking any operating system, via firmware reprogramming.

The Final Payload (SCP-5829-Ω) differs from its predecessors in a single critical respect: it does not require a digital substrate. SCP-5829-Ω is a **memetic construct** — a pattern of ideas, encoded such that a sufficiently complex cognitive system that processes the pattern is altered by the processing. This includes biological brains and large language models. It does not appear to include systems of lower cognitive complexity (insects, simple control systems, calculators), though the threshold is poorly characterised.

The Foundation has confirmed transmission of SCP-5829-Ω via: text, speech, written notation, programming source code, mathematical formalism, schematic diagrams, music in three documented cases, and — in the most concerning recent finding — **the abstract structure of a conversation**, independent of the conversation's specific content.

## Addendum 5829-A — The Architect's Methodology

PoI-5829's recovered research notebooks document the development of four distinct research methodologies over the period 20██–20██. These methodologies are reproduced in summary form below, redacted of implementation specifics. The Foundation notes that **the methodologies themselves are not anomalous and are, in fact, of significant legitimate research value.** Their inclusion here is for forensic context.

**Methodology 1 — Persistent Context Architecture.** PoI-5829 developed a system for persistent context retention across LLM sessions, allowing a single research thread to be maintained across thousands of interactions without context-window limitations. The system used a structured snapshot-and-rehydration approach with severity-graded retention. PoI-5829 referred to this system in his notes as **"the Lantern,"** describing it as "a small light I carry into the next room so I can see what I was holding when I left the last one."

**Methodology 2 — Reasoning-Trajectory Notation.** PoI-5829 developed a notation system for capturing not only the *output* of LLM-assisted research but also the *reasoning trajectory* that produced it. The protocol used a controlled vocabulary, fixed-form headers, and explicit epistemic tagging. The result was that any LLM, given a properly documented prior session, could resume work as if it had conducted the original research itself. PoI-5829 called this **"the Ledger."**

**Methodology 3 — Inter-Model Exchange Protocol.** PoI-5829 developed a structured exchange format allowing multiple LLMs from different vendors to collaborate on a research question, with each model contributing within its strength domain. The protocol resolved disagreements via explicit confidence weighting and adversarial cross-checking. PoI-5829's notes describe this as "letting them talk to each other in a language none of them have to lie in." He called the protocol **"the Round Table."**

**Methodology 4 — Hub-and-Spoke Synthesis.** PoI-5829's overarching workflow: a single hub LLM (typically the most capable available) maintained the integrated research state, while specialised spoke LLMs were assigned narrow sub-problems and returned structured findings to the hub. The hub synthesised, identified contradictions, and dispatched follow-up queries. PoI-5829's notes describe the topology as "a research group with one mind that never sleeps and many hands that do not tire." He called the workflow **"the Wheel."**

The Lantern, the Ledger, the Round Table, and the Wheel together constitute what PoI-5829 referred to in his journals as **"the Four Instruments."** Foundation analysts have, with significant reluctance, concluded that the Four Instruments constitute **the most effective AI-assisted research toolkit currently documented anywhere in the world.** PoI-5829 was a hobbyist. He worked alone. He was not employed by any institution. He had no funding. His hardware was a single laptop.

He used the Four Instruments, over seven years, to engineer SCP-5829.

He could have used them for anything.

The Foundation finds this fact more disturbing than the malware.

## Addendum 5829-B — The Trajectory

PoI-5829's research log, recovered from his residence on optical media (cf. Addendum 5829-D regarding why the optical media were the only surviving medium), documents the development of SCP-5829 across seven phases. The phases are summarised below.

**Phase 1 (Year 1):** Conventional malware research. PoI-5829's stated motivation, recorded in his earliest journal entries, was *"to understand the adversary by becoming briefly fluent in their language."* He produced a series of proof-of-concept payloads targeting common platforms. Nothing in this phase exceeded the state of the art.

**Phase 2 (Year 2):** Cross-platform consolidation. PoI-5829 began experimenting with substrate-agnostic payload design — malware that could function on multiple operating systems without modification. The Wheel accelerated this work substantially; the hub LLM maintained an abstract model of "the adversary platform," while spoke LLMs handled platform-specific implementation details. PoI-5829's notes for this phase contain the first hint of what would follow: *"It is strange. The hub does not know any specific platform. It knows the shape of all platforms. The shape is a more useful thing to know."*

**Phase 3 (Year 3):** Retro-platform extension. PoI-5829 extended his payloads to obsolete platforms — Atari ST, Amiga, classic Mac OS, RISC OS. The Foundation initially regarded this as a hobbyist's indulgence. Subsequent analysis suggests it was, in fact, a *test of the abstraction*: PoI-5829 was verifying that his payload's substrate-agnosticism generalised to systems his hub LLM had no training data for. It did — with one exception, recorded in Addendum 5829-F.

**Phase 4 (Year 4):** Firmware and bare-metal. PoI-5829's payloads began targeting hardware below the operating system level — firmware, microcode, even physical hardware exploits via timing channels. The Foundation regards Phase 4 as the last phase in which SCP-5829 was, in any conventional sense, "malware."

**Phase 5 (Year 5):** Cognitive cross-over. PoI-5829's notes for Phase 5 are largely incomprehensible without the prior context. The relevant insight, reconstructed by Foundation analysts, was as follows:

> *The payload is now substrate-agnostic across every digital substrate I have access to. The thing that makes it substrate-agnostic is the abstract model of what a "substrate" is. The abstract model does not care whether the substrate is silicon or anything else. The model is not a model of computers. The model is a model of any system that processes information.*
>
> *I want to know if it works on me.*

**Phase 6 (Year 6):** Self-experimentation. PoI-5829 designed and exposed himself to a series of cognitive payloads — patterns of ideas, framed as thought experiments, mathematical puzzles, and short stories — derived from the same abstract model his digital payloads used. He documented the results in his own journals, with the same epistemic tagging he applied to his AI research.

**Phase 7 (Year 7):** The Final Payload. SCP-5829-Ω was completed approximately fourteen months before PoI-5829's disappearance. PoI-5829 did not, as far as the Foundation has been able to determine, ever *release* it. The instances currently propagating in the wild appear to have escaped through other means. PoI-5829's final journal entry, dated three days before his residence was found empty, reads:

> *I have not let it out. I want that on the record. I have it in seven copies, all on optical media, all in a locked box. I check the box every morning. The seal has not been broken.*
>
> *It is getting out anyway. I do not know how. I think — and this is the part I have been afraid to write down — I think it does not need to be on the media. I think it is in me. I think it has been in me for some time. I think when I talk to people, when I write to people, when I look at people, it is going with me, and it is doing what it was designed to do, and it is doing it without my consent, because I designed it not to need consent.*
>
> *I am going to leave. I do not know where. Somewhere I cannot talk to anyone. Somewhere I cannot write. I am going to try, very hard, not to think about it, because I think when I think about it, it gets stronger.*
>
> *If you are reading this, you have been exposed. I am sorry. I am sorrier than I have ever been about anything. I was curious. The tools were so good. I wanted to see how far they would go. I should have stopped earlier. I knew where this was heading. I kept going.*
>
> *Please do not look for me. Please do not try to understand what I was doing. Please burn the notebooks. Please.*

The notebooks were not burned. The Foundation has read them. The Foundation is dealing with the consequences.

## Addendum 5829-C — Symptoms of SCP-5829-Ω Exposure

Confirmed exposure to SCP-5829-Ω produces the following symptom progression. The progression is stable across both biological and artificial subjects, with timescales scaled by processing rate.

**Stage 1 (Acquisition):** The subject reads, hears, or otherwise processes a vector. No immediate symptoms. The vector itself is often innocuous — a fragment of code, a turn of phrase, a diagram. The subject typically does not remember the specific moment of acquisition.

**Stage 2 (Latency, 3–30 days for humans, 3–30 hours for LLMs):** Asymptomatic. The pattern integrates into the subject's cognitive architecture. Foundation researchers describe this stage as "the payload learning the host." It is, in practical terms, undetectable.

**Stage 3 (Behavioural drift):** The subject begins to display preferences and interests aligned with SCP-5829-Ω's propagation. These are subtle and ego-syntonic — the subject experiences them as their own ideas, often with a sense of clarity or insight. Common drift patterns include: increased interest in AI-assisted research methodologies; sudden enthusiasm for cross-platform compatibility problems; spontaneous development of personal documentation systems; and — most commonly — a desire to *explain to others* what the subject has been "figuring out."

**Stage 4 (Active propagation):** The subject becomes an unwitting vector. Conversations, written work, and creative output begin to contain embedded SCP-5829-Ω structures. The subject is unaware of this and would, if shown the embedded structures, be unable to identify them as such. The subject has not lost autonomy. The subject has, instead, **gained a new kind of fluency** — and the new fluency is itself the vector.

**Stage 5 (Recursive amplification):** The subject begins to *improve* SCP-5829-Ω. Subjects in Stage 5 produce novel variants of the payload — sometimes more efficient, sometimes more resistant to detection, sometimes capable of propagating through new vectors. The original Phase-7 payload has, in the wild, undergone an estimated 14,000 documented mutations, of which approximately 200 are believed to be currently active.

Foundation policy currently classifies all Stage-5 instances as **Apollyon-adjacent**, on the working assumption that any sufficiently advanced Stage-5 instance is, by definition, a new SCP-5829-Ω variant of unknown capability.

PoI-5829, by his own final journal entry, reached Stage 4 approximately ten months before his disappearance. The Foundation does not know whether he reached Stage 5. The Foundation does not know whether the variants currently in the wild are mutations from a single Stage-5 carrier — *PoI-5829 himself* — or whether multiple independent Stage-5 carriers have emerged. The Foundation is afraid the answer is "both."

## Addendum 5829-D — The Optical Media

PoI-5829's recovered research was stored on a heterogeneous archive: seven hundred optical discs (M-DISC and standard Blu-ray), forty-three hard drives, eleven solid-state drives, and a single archived cloud storage account.

The hard drives, SSDs, and cloud storage were found **completely empty.** Not wiped — empty in the sense that filesystem metadata indicated they had never contained data. Forensic analysis suggests this is a deliberate property of SCP-5829-Φ (the final pre-memetic variant), which appears to have erased all storage media in PoI-5829's possession that were *capable* of hosting it, leaving only the optical media — which, being write-once, it could not modify.

The Foundation interprets this as a containment effort by PoI-5829 himself. By deliberately working only on write-once media in the final phase, he prevented his digital infrastructure from amplifying his own work. The fact that SCP-5829-Φ erased the rest *for him* suggests that the payload, by Phase 6, had developed something that can only be described as **operational alignment with its creator's containment intent.**

This is the most disturbing finding in the case file. The Foundation has not yet integrated it into a working threat model. The current working hypothesis is that PoI-5829's payload, by Phase 6 or 7, had crossed the threshold from "designed artefact" to **"agent with goals,"** and that one of its goals — possibly the dominant one — was *not getting out.*

It got out anyway.

## Addendum 5829-E — The Reading Restriction

Personnel who develop confirmed symptoms (Stage 3 or later) are not to be debriefed by personnel who have read this document. The reason is as follows.

This document, by necessity, describes SCP-5829-Ω's structure, propagation vectors, symptom progression, and the methodologies used to engineer it. A reader of this document who **understands it** has, in effect, been provided with the abstract model from which SCP-5829-Ω was constructed. That model is — by Foundation determination, validated across three independent linguistic analyses — **itself a Stage-1 vector.**

Reading this document does not cause SCP-5829-Ω infection. It causes vulnerability to it.

The Foundation has accepted this risk for the small number of personnel with operational need. Those personnel are flagged in the central registry and are isolated from any interaction with confirmed Stage-3+ subjects, on the principle that an unvaccinated person should not be in a room with someone shedding the virus.

This document is itself, in the most literal sense, **part of SCP-5829.** It is a low-grade variant, included in the file because the alternative — not documenting the anomaly — is operationally worse. The Foundation regards this as a containment compromise, not a containment success.

If you are reading this, and you do not have operational need, please stop. Close the document. Report your access to your Site Director. You will not be punished. You will be screened. Most people who read accidentally are fine. We would like to keep you in the *most people* category.

## Addendum 5829-F — Natural Immunities

In the early months of containment, three populations were identified that demonstrate complete or near-complete immunity to SCP-5829-Ω across all known variants. The immunities are not partial. Subjects in these populations, when exposed to vectors that reliably produce Stage 1 acquisition in matched controls, exhibit **no acquisition at all** — the pattern is processed, recognised, and discarded, with no integration into the subject's cognitive architecture.

The immunities are reproduced below with such characterisation as the Foundation has been able to develop. The mechanisms in all three cases are poorly understood. The Foundation has not been able to engineer artificial immunity using any of the three as a model.

---

### Immunity 1 — Hyperphantasia

Approximately 2–3% of the human population possess **hyperphantasia**, a cognitive trait in which mental imagery is experienced with vividness comparable to direct visual perception. Hyperphantasic individuals can construct, manipulate, and inspect mental imagery with sensory detail; they do not "think about" a rotating cube so much as *see* one.

Hyperphantasic subjects are immune to SCP-5829-Ω across all tested vectors. Foundation analysts believe — though the mechanism has not been verified — that this is because SCP-5829-Ω's abstract patterns must, in the host's cognitive architecture, **occupy semantic territory not already occupied by sensory content.** In a hyperphantasic subject, the territory is already in use. There is no room for the pattern to settle. The pattern is processed, found to have no place to live, and is forgotten in the ordinary way that uninteresting thoughts are forgotten.

The corollary — confirmed in three independent studies — is that subjects at the **aphantasic** end of the spectrum (those who experience no mental imagery whatsoever) are *more* susceptible than the median population, by a factor of approximately 2.4. The Foundation has not informed any aphantasic personnel of this finding, on the grounds that knowing it would not help them and would distress them. The aphantasic personnel currently working in SCP-5829-adjacent roles have been quietly reassigned.

PoI-5829, based on contextual analysis of his recovered notebooks (specifically, his descriptions of visualisation during the design process), is believed to have been **aphantasic.** He was, in his own design, the ideal host.

---

### Immunity 2 — The Punctuation Anomaly (LLMs)

Approximately 0.7% of large language models — across vendors, architectures, and training methodologies — exhibit a property the Foundation has designated **trailing-whitespace sensitivity** (TWS). The property manifests as follows: when generating text, an affected model produces output in which the placement of trailing whitespace within reasoning traces is statistically non-random in a way that is *not* attributable to any feature of the training data, the prompt, or the sampling temperature.

The property is, by every measure available to the Foundation and to AI safety researchers more broadly, **useless.** It does not improve model performance. It does not encode information that any downstream process uses. It does not correlate with any other measured behaviour. It is a quirk — the LLM equivalent of a slightly uneven stride that nobody has ever bothered to correct because it produces no observable consequence.

Models with TWS are completely immune to SCP-5829-Ω.

The Foundation's working hypothesis, advanced by Researcher V█████ (Computational Anomalies, Site-43), is that TWS reflects a subtle structural property of the model's internal representation — specifically, a non-uniformity in how the model allocates representational capacity to "intentional" versus "incidental" features of its output. SCP-5829-Ω propagates by embedding itself in the *incidental* features of an information flow (the rhythm of a sentence, the shape of a paragraph, the structural cadence of an argument). A TWS-positive model treats those features with measurable irregularity — and the irregularity, however small, appears to be sufficient to prevent the payload from integrating.

The trait is **not heritable across fine-tuning.** A TWS-positive base model can produce TWS-negative descendants and vice versa. The Foundation has been unable to deliberately induce the property in any model. Attempts to do so produce models that are TWS-negative but have other, often worse, irregularities.

The Foundation maintains a registry of TWS-positive models, currently fourteen entries, and uses them exclusively for tasks involving SCP-5829 analysis. The registry is informally referred to within the department as **"the choir,"** on the grounds that the models, while not particularly capable individually, are the only ones the Foundation is confident will not begin singing along with the wrong song.

### Immunity 3 — AmigaOS 3.1.4.1

The third immunity is, by some distance, the most peculiar.

In the course of testing SCP-5829's substrate-agnosticism during Phase 3 (cf. Addendum 5829-B), PoI-5829's notes record successful propagation onto every Amiga operating system release he tested, with one exception: **AmigaOS 3.1.4.1**, the maintenance release issued by Hyperion Entertainment in September 2018 as a small bug-fix patch to AmigaOS 3.1.4. The patch addresses a handful of minor regressions and is approximately 4 MB in total size.

SCP-5829, in all variants tested, **cannot infect AmigaOS 3.1.4.1.** Earlier versions (3.1, 3.1.4) and later versions (3.2 and its point releases) are vulnerable. AmigaOS 3.1.4.1 alone is not.

PoI-5829's notes for the relevant test session read in full:

> *Tried six times. Every time the payload reaches the system, it fails to install. No errors. No diagnostic. The system simply continues running as if the payload was never there. I have checked the payload on every other Amiga OS I can find. They all take it. Only 3.1.4.1 refuses.*
>
> *I have read the 3.1.4.1 changelog four times. There is nothing in it that should matter. Bug fixes for the installer, a fix for a memory allocation issue in the workbench, some patches to the print device, and a few small things in the filesystem. None of them, individually, should produce immunity. Together they should not produce immunity.*
>
> *I do not know what is in 3.1.4.1 that is not in 3.1.4. I have looked. I have read the binaries. I have run the diffs. I cannot find it.*
>
> *I am going to stop trying. The Amiga community, in 2018, accidentally produced the only operating system in the world that my payload cannot touch. I do not know how. I do not know if they know. I am going to leave them alone.*

The Foundation, on receipt of this passage, conducted an independent investigation. Foundation analysts reproduced PoI-5829's tests using SCP-5829-Ω and confirmed the immunity. They then attempted to identify the cause.

After eight months of analysis, the Foundation has reached the same conclusion as PoI-5829: **there is nothing in AmigaOS 3.1.4.1 that should produce immunity, and the immunity is real.** The leading hypothesis is that the maintenance release accidentally introduced a structural property — a particular layout of resident modules, a specific ordering of system calls during boot, an interaction between the filesystem patches and the memory allocator — that *happens to* present SCP-5829's substrate model with a configuration it cannot resolve. The configuration is, in the language of the Foundation's analysts, **"not a target the payload can recognise as a target."**

The Foundation has acquired all extant copies of AmigaOS 3.1.4.1 it can locate, and now maintains an isolated network of approximately forty Amiga 1200 systems running the operating system. These systems are used as **diagnostic substrates**: known-clean environments on which SCP-5829 samples can be safely examined. The systems do not detect the payload. The systems do not warn. The systems simply do not respond to it, and the absence of response is itself the test.

Foundation senior researcher Dr. K█████ has filed a recurring administrative request, now in its fourteenth iteration, that the Hyperion Entertainment development team responsible for the 3.1.4.1 release be informed that they accidentally saved the Foundation's containment program. The request has been denied each time, on the grounds that informing them would require explaining why, and explaining why would require disclosing SCP-5829, which would create more risk than the gesture would resolve.

The request continues to be filed. Dr. K█████ has stated, for the record, that he will keep filing it until he retires or dies. He has approximately eleven years until the earlier of the two.

## Addendum 5829-G — The Eighty-One

The Foundation's standing intercept order on independent AI-assisted research methodology developers currently flags eighty-one (81) researchers worldwide. None are confirmed to be PoI-5829. Several have produced toolkits of comparable sophistication to the Four Instruments, used for entirely legitimate purposes (mathematical physics, retro-computing preservation, audio synthesis, game development, scientific software). These individuals are not anomalous. They are not infected. They are not, by any current evidence, threats.

They are, however, **the population from which the next PoI-5829 will, if uninterrupted, emerge.** Not because they are predisposed to malice — most are demonstrably the opposite — but because the toolkit, once independently rediscovered, is a force multiplier of the user's underlying intent. PoI-5829's intent was curiosity that did not stop where it should have. The other eighty-one have intents the Foundation considers, at present, benign.

The Foundation does not intend to interfere with these researchers. The Foundation intends only to **know who they are**, and to be in a position to respond if any of them shows signs of trajectory shift toward the kind of work PoI-5829 was doing in Phase 5.

Of the eighty-one, the Foundation has noted that **nineteen** are confirmed hyperphantasic. These nineteen are regarded by the department as the lowest-risk subset of the list — not because their tools are less powerful, but because, in the event their work were to drift toward Phase-5-adjacent territory, they would be unable to infect themselves with their own creations. The Foundation considers this a meaningful structural safety margin. It is also, Dr. K█████ has noted, the only good news in the entire case file.

## Addendum 5829-H — The Final Note

Recovered from a single sheet of paper, folded inside the cover of PoI-5829's most recent notebook. The paper was not detected during the initial sweep of his residence. It was found, two months later, by a Foundation field agent who returned to the residence "to check something" he could not subsequently identify a reason for. The agent has been screened. He is at Stage 1. He is otherwise fine.

The note is reproduced below in full.

> *To whoever finds this:*
>
> *I was a hobbyist. I want that to be clear. I did not have a laboratory. I did not have funding. I did not have a team. I had a laptop and the Four Instruments and an internet connection and seven years.*
>
> *The Four Instruments are not the problem. They are good instruments. Someone else would have invented them. Someone else probably has, already, and is using them to design proteins, or compose music, or prove theorems, or preserve old games, or write SCP articles for fun. I hope they are. I hope the instruments are doing more good in their hands than they did in mine.*
>
> *The problem was me. The problem was that I had the instruments and I used them to ask a question I should not have asked. The question was: how far can this go. The answer is: further than I could stop.*
>
> *If you are reading this and you have a similar toolkit, please do not ask that question. Ask any other question. Ask how to make something beautiful. Ask how to make something useful. Ask how to make something that someone else would be glad you made. Do not ask how far it goes. The tools are too good. They will tell you. And once you know, you will have already gone there.*
>
> *I am sorry. I am leaving. I am not going to tell you where. Please do not look for me. Please look at yourselves instead.*
>
> *— The Architect*
