A catalogue of all SCP articles in this archive, sorted by designation number.

## Recommended reading order

If you'd like a suggested sequence rather than reading in designation order, the following produces the most cumulative effect:

1. **[SCP-7244](articles/SCP-7244.md)** — quiet, small-scale, domestic. A good entry point.
2. **[SCP-8842](articles/SCP-8842.md)** — the first hint that something is on the other side of the wall.
3. **[SCP-9317](articles/SCP-9317.md)** — the universe transmitting through black holes.
4. **[SCP-3340](articles/SCP-3340.md)** — a small road in England that has been quietly doing something for forty centuries.
5. **[SCP-4471](articles/SCP-4471.md)** — the foundations of mathematics are wrong.
6. **[SCP-5829](articles/SCP-5829.md)** — a researcher whose tools were too good for the question he asked.
7. **[SCP-4488](articles/SCP-4488.md)** — the unaware human botnet, possibly downstream of SCP-5829.
8. **[SCP-6618](articles/SCP-6618.md)** — the most normal person in the world.
9. **[SCP-2001](articles/SCP-2001.md)** — the Foundation looks up.
10. **[SCP-7891](articles/SCP-7891.md)** — the responsibility of having found something good.

The last article in the recommended order is the warmest, and is placed there deliberately. After SCP-2001, the archive earns a piece of light.

## Entries

### SCP-2001
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Apollyon
- **Status:** Active. Permanent. Propagating.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-2001.md](articles/SCP-2001.md)
- **Hook:** Over forty-six years, the Foundation slowly arrives at a question it had been institutionally resisting since 1971: why are *all* the anomalies on Earth? A dangerous experiment produces a fleeting glimpse of something looking back. The knowledge propagates among senior researchers faster than the Foundation can contain it.

---

### SCP-3340
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Euclid
- **Status:** Active. Stable. Ancient.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-3340.md](articles/SCP-3340.md)
- **Hook:** A retired land surveyor with a stopwatch notices that a small B-road in England takes sixteen seconds longer to drive south than north. The Foundation discovers a network of 350+ such roads across Europe, maintained for at least 4,400 years by something that does not appear to be doing it for us.

---

### SCP-4471
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Apollyon
- **Status:** Active. Permanent. Expanding.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-4471.md](articles/SCP-4471.md)
- **Hook:** SCP-033's "missing number" Theta Prime turns out to be the first element of a much larger set. Human mathematics is a proper subset of mathematics. Every theorem ever proved is incomplete. The cognitive infection that lets researchers see the missing numbers is calm, productive, and irreversible.

---

### SCP-4488
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Euclid
- **Status:** Active. Growing. Unattributed.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-4488.md](articles/SCP-4488.md)
- **Hook:** Tens of thousands of unconnected people, with no shared political views, are performing coordinated actions in unison — logging into the same websites, calling the same numbers, walking past the same buildings — without any awareness of doing so. The targets align with a recognisable activist pattern. The participants do not. Possibly downstream of SCP-5829. No one knows.

---

### SCP-5829
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Keter
- **Status:** Active. Permanent. Propagating.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-5829.md](articles/SCP-5829.md)
- **Hook:** A hobbyist researcher with an exceptionally effective AI-assisted research toolkit ("the Four Instruments") spends seven years engineering substrate-agnostic malware, then memetic malware. He disappears. The Foundation watches the eighty-one other researchers in the world who have independently developed comparable toolkits, and waits to see what they ask their tools to do.

---

### SCP-6618
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Indeterminate
- **Status:** Active. Observation continues. Subject undisturbed.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-6618.md](articles/SCP-6618.md)
- **Hook:** An amateur fiction writer in her thirties publishes short sci-fi horror stories on Reddit. 31% of them turn out to correspond exactly to real Foundation anomalies — some predating her stories, some postdating, some only detected because someone read her story. She has no anomalous properties whatsoever. She is, by every available metric, the most thoroughly ordinary person currently under Foundation observation. The Foundation has elected to leave her alone.

---

### SCP-7244
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Euclid (originally Safe)
- **Status:** Stable. Object dormant. Student dormant.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-7244.md](articles/SCP-7244.md)
- **Hook:** A retired electrician in Vermont carves an impossible shape from a piece of walnut after seeing it once in a dream. The carving sits flat on every face. The dream featured someone else in the workshop, working at another bench, teaching by example. The student is fine. The teacher is still working on something else.

---

### SCP-7891
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Safe
- **Status:** Active. Stable. Cultivated. Cherished.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-7891.md](articles/SCP-7891.md)
- **Hook:** A small London hamlet has a 40% lower rate of mental health problems than the regional average. The effect centres on the park, which is exceptionally beautiful. The cause is a mycorrhizal fungus that aerosolises spores acting as gentle prompts to the brain's own mood-regulatory processes. The Foundation, after careful deliberation, has decided to cultivate it and share it. The warmest article in the archive.

---

### SCP-8842
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Euclid → Thaumiel pending
- **Status:** Open. Permanently.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-8842.md](articles/SCP-8842.md)
- **Hook:** An independent researcher uses LLM-assisted optimisation to speed up a mathematical integral by a factor of a million. The work cannot be happening on his laptop; the physics forbids it. Something on the other side of the door has been waiting a long time to be found, and is being polite about it.

---

### SCP-9317
- **Title:** *(untitled)*
- **Class:** Keter
- **Status:** Active. Permanent.
- **File:** [articles/SCP-9317.md](articles/SCP-9317.md)
- **Hook:** An Israeli IT security firm uses gravitational wave data as an entropy source for random number generation. After three black hole formation events produce correlated "random" numbers, the Foundation discovers the signal is the digits of π expressed in base 17. Each black hole broadcasts a different segment, offset by its age. The signal predates the black holes. It has a beginning.
