An archive of original SCP Foundation articles, written for fun.

These are not canon entries on the [SCP Wiki](https://scpwiki.com). They are independent pieces written in the SCP format, drafted collaboratively and kept here as a personal collection. If you stumbled across this repo and you enjoy the SCP format, you are welcome to read.

## What is the SCP Foundation?

The [SCP Foundation](https://scpwiki.com) is a long-running collaborative fiction project. Contributors write in-universe documents describing anomalous objects, entities, and phenomena that a fictional secret organisation ("the Foundation") has identified, classified, and is attempting to contain. The format is constrained — clinical tone, redactions, addenda, recovered documents, interview transcripts — and the constraints are part of the appeal. Done well, an SCP article reads like a leaked classified file about something that should not exist.

The articles in this repo follow the format. They do not share continuity with the official SCP Wiki except where they explicitly reference established articles (e.g. SCP-033).

## Reading the archive

There are two ways to read.

**On the live site at [darian-frey.github.io/archive-19](https://darian-frey.github.io/archive-19/)** — a single-page terminal-themed reader with four colour modes (phosphor green, amber, white, and parchment) and four typefaces. The articles render in a clinical archive interface that suits the format. This is the recommended way to read.

**Directly on GitHub** — open the [`articles/`](articles/) directory and read each `.md` file in GitHub's native Markdown view. Plain, functional, no styling beyond GitHub's defaults. Useful if you want to read or copy a specific article without the surrounding interface.

## Catalogue

The full catalogue of articles in this archive is maintained in **[INDEX.md](INDEX.md)**, sorted by designation number, with a short hook for each entry. Start there if you're looking for something specific or want a feel for the collection.

## Reading order

The articles can be read in any order. If you'd like a suggested sequence, INDEX.md notes a recommended reading order at the top of the file.

A common thread runs through them: institutions, researchers, and ordinary people encountering the limits of what they can know about themselves and the universe, and the cost — borne by individuals — of holding that knowledge. Not every article in the archive is dark. Some are about the responsibility of having found something good.

## Format

Articles are written in Markdown and render natively on GitHub. The format follows SCP Wiki conventions:

- Item number, object class, special containment procedures, and description at the top
- Addenda for additional information (interviews, incidents, recovered documents, statements)
- Redaction notation (`█████`, `[REDACTED]`) for in-universe censorship
- Block quotes for personnel statements, recovered notes, and dialogue
- Code-style formatting for entity dialogue where appropriate

## A note on canon

These articles use the SCP Foundation as a setting and the SCP article format as a structural device. They are written with affection for the original project and are not intended to compete with, parody, or replace canonical wiki entries. If they entertain anyone besides me, that's a bonus.
